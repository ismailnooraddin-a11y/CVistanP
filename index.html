Common questions

Good to know before you visit.

How do I book an appointment? +

Booking is done by WhatsApp. You can either tap any "Book on WhatsApp" button, choose a specific doctor from the Doctors section, or fill in the short request form — we'll open WhatsApp with your details pre-filled.

Do I have to choose a treatment first? +

No. Many patients don't know exactly what treatment they need — which is normal. Just send your name and preferred time, and the doctor's team will guide you from there.

Why are there three WhatsApp numbers? +

Our 16 specialists are organized across three direct lines, each shared by a group of doctors. When you choose a doctor in our Doctors section, the correct line is selected automatically.

What are your working hours? +

Saturday through Thursday, 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Friday closed. Messages received outside hours will be answered first thing the next working day.

Where exactly is the clinic? +

32 Park / 100m Road, near Sarbasti, Erbil Governorate, 44001. Open in Google Maps →

Do you treat children? +

Yes. We treat children, teens, and adults across general, orthodontic, and specialist care. Please mention the patient's age when you message us so we route you to the right doctor.
